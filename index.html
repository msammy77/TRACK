UKMLA & MCCQE Study Tracker

Use this lightweight app to tick off your daily study blocks for each week of the UKMLA‑first plan, with MCCQE/NAC practice layered in. Simply pick the week you are on and cross off blocks as you complete them.

Overall completion: 0%

How to use

  • Each day has up to four blocks: UKMLA questions, content review, OSCE/CPSA practice, and MCCQE/NAC questions.
  • Tick a box when you finish that block; the day and weekly progress update automatically.
  • Return to this page during the day to keep yourself accountable; progress is kept in this session only.

Tip: Bookmark this page on your phone or laptop and open it alongside your question bank or OSCE practice so you can cross off blocks as you go.
